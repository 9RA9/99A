About the scheme

One year.
A shared experience.

The National Youth Service Corps is a national service scheme created to encourage common ties among Nigerian youths and promote national unity. Its work brings young graduates into shared experiences of service, development and community.

The NYSC's official materials describe its mission around national unity, sustainable development, self-reliance and preparing graduate youths for leadership. Read the official overview →

The service year

Four stages.
One journey.

01

Orientation

Begin the service year with the compulsory orientation course and shared camp experience.

02

03

04
